# Peppol Suite — Company & Tax Launch Checklist

Internal execution checklist. This records open professional-confirmation items; it is not legal or tax advice.

## Before commercial operation

- [ ] Confirm Belgian legal entity type and incorporation path.
- [ ] Confirm ownership/control structure and UBO registration.
- [ ] Confirm director/active-partner eligibility and any professional-card or self-employed requirements that apply.
- [ ] Confirm VAT registration and invoicing obligations.
- [ ] Confirm Belgian corporate-tax and bookkeeping obligations.
- [ ] Confirm founder employment agreement and employer policies permit the activity.
- [ ] Confirm IP ownership/licensing and that company development does not use employer time, equipment, confidential information or code.
- [ ] Confirm remuneration/dividend/related-party treatment with a Belgian accountant/tax adviser.
- [ ] Confirm cross-border India/Belgium tax and reporting treatment where relevant.
- [ ] Confirm business bank/payment-account requirements before live payment activation.

## Founder decision gates

- Working direction: Belgian operating company with the Founder’s spouse as intended owner/operator, subject to professional confirmation.
- Live payments must remain disabled until the legal/company/tax gates are confirmed and the Founder explicitly authorizes activation.

## Evidence rule

A code change or GitHub CI result cannot substitute for incorporation, tax, employment/IP or regulatory advice. Record the professional adviser, date and supporting document when each item is confirmed.
